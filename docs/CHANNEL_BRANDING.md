# Channel branding plan (drafted 2026-11-21)

## What the channel looks like now (public page, checked 2026-11-21)
- Name **"HustleHub USA"**, handle `@kenchin-x1d` (random), 5 public videos.
- The description is still the old money/hustle blurb ("home of smart hustlers and future millionaires"). It does not match our story Shorts.
- Four of the five public Shorts are the old GTA 6 and money content (2–26 views). Only one is a story. No custom icon or banner.
- A mixed channel (money + GTA + stories) confuses YouTube's recommendation system, so the channel should be rebranded as **one niche: original animated stories**.

| 1 | **Tiny Giant Stories** | Nico's smiling face, cyan bolt tee, spiky blond hair, gold ring around it | The cast lined up in front of the gym, Nico small in the middle, a big shadow of a giant behind him | Small kids. Big moments. |

| 4 | **Blocky Tales** | A warm music-box icon with a blocky heart (matches our music-box tunes) | A cosy storybook scene: open book with blocky characters standing up out of the pages | Little stories with a big heart. |

**Recommendation: #1 Tiny Giant Stories.** It is easy to say and remember, and it fits our two best performers (the underdog stories). It is not tied to one story, so it can hold new formats, and the name is not a copy of a competitor (check availability on YouTube before applying; do not use any competitor's name).
