## 2. Customization → Profile → Banner and Picture
You said you already have the banner and the profile picture. Upload them there. Banner: 2560×1440, keep text/faces inside the centre 1546×423. Picture: 800×800.

## 3. Settings → Channel → Basic info
- **Country:** United States
- **Keywords** (paste as one line):
```
animated stories, story time animation, rule world, strange rules, plot twist, school story, underdog story, animated short stories, blocky animation, story shorts
```

- **Description** (default for any manual upload):

```
- **Tags:**
```
rule world stories, animated story, story time animation, roblox story, plot twist

- **Advanced → Language:** English. **Comments:** On (hold potentially inappropriate comments for review).

## 8. Pinned comment (post on each new Short, from your own account)
- Ask one question per video; the SEO agent writes one per episode in `output/<id>/qa/seo.json` (field `pinned_comment`).
  Example for episode 5: "Nico broke his promise to his mum to prove himself. Would you have done the same? Tell me below."

## 9. Notes
- YouTube limits name and handle changes (about 2 per 14 days), so choose once.
- A synthetic-content label: our narrator voice is AI. For stylised animation that nobody could mistake for real footage, YouTube normally does not require the "altered or synthetic content" label, but when Studio asks at upload, answer honestly.
- Nothing I do uploads to Studio automatically; only videos are scheduled.
