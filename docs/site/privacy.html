Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

This tool is used by one person, the owner of the Rule World Stories YouTube channel, to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to read the owner's own channel statistics through the YouTube Data API.

  • It collects no data from visitors and from other users, and it has no other users.
  • The Google sign-in token of the channel owner is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's own accounts. It is never shared, sold or used for anything else.
  • Access to the owner's Google account can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
