## Context and rule
Explain only what viewers need to understand the situation.

## Character goal and first consequence
Show what the protagonist wants and how the rule gets in the way.

## Escalation
Each scene must add a new consequence, reveal information or force a harder choice.

## Major choice / reveal
Make the protagonist act. Do not resolve the story through coincidence alone.

## Payoff / emotional landing
Resolve the opening promise. Leave a memorable image or line.

## Per-scene production fields
For each scene provide:
- Scene ID and time range
- Location and time of day
- Characters on screen
- Character action and blocking
- Facial expression at start and end
- Camera shot and movement
- Lighting and atmosphere
- Exact spoken dialogue
- Narrator delivery direction
- Sound cues
- Continuity constraints
- Generation prompt

## Final checks
- The hook is understandable immediately.
- The protagonist wants something specific.
- Stakes are personal and visible.
- No scene repeats information without adding tension.
- Dialogue sounds like something a person would say.
- The ending pays off the premise.
- The idea is not a thin rewrite of an existing upload.
