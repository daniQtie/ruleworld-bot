# Story Formula and Hook Library

## The core formula

**Strange rule + personal stake + escalating consequences + earned payoff.**

A world rule is only a premise. It becomes a story when a specific character wants something, has something to lose, and must make difficult choices because of the rule.

## Eight-beat story model

1. **Hook (0–3 sec):** Show the rule or its shocking consequence immediately.
2. **Context (3–15 sec):** Explain only what the audience needs to understand.
3. **Character goal:** Make the protagonist's immediate desire clear.
4. **First consequence:** Show how the rule affects the protagonist.
5. **Escalation:** Each new scene changes the situation or makes it harder.
6. **Choice:** The protagonist must choose between competing needs.
7. **Reveal/payoff:** Resolve or reframe the opening premise.
8. **Emotional landing:** End on a feeling, consequence or memorable image.

## Hook principles

- Start with a concrete event, not a generic introduction.
- State the unusual rule in plain language.
- Show the consequence visually as early as possible.
- Create a question that the story will answer.
- Avoid misleading hooks that the story never pays off.
- Do not begin with “Imagine a world...”, “Have you ever wondered...”, or “Once upon a time...” unless there is a strong creative reason.

## Original hook patterns

These are structures, not finished copy-paste titles:

1. “In this town, every promise leaves a mark...”
2. “The first person to fall asleep disappears from the family photo...”
3. “Every door shows a day you wish you could change...”
4. “In this school, your reflection answers before you do...”
5. “Everyone receives one warning before their luck runs out...”
6. “Every time someone forgets you, a light goes out...”
7. “In this town, the youngest person decides everyone's future...”
8. “Each family has one room nobody is allowed to enter...”
9. “Every secret appears as a tiny shadow...”
10. “The town's clock stops whenever someone tells the truth...”
11. “Everyone can hear your thoughts, except one person...”
12. “Every birthday removes one memory, but nobody knows which one...”
13. “Your shadow can leave, but only when you lie...”
14. “Every apology must be paid back with a memory...”
15. “In this town, lost things come home with something extra...”
16. “Everyone receives a letter from their future self, except me...”
17. “The streetlights turn off whenever someone is hiding...”
18. “Every time you say goodbye, the person moves one step farther away...”
19. “In this school, the quietest student controls the bell...”
20. “Everyone gets one chance to trade places with their future self...”

## Story originality gate

Before approving an idea, compare it to the last 20 published scripts. Reject or substantially redesign ideas that merely swap nouns in an existing formula. Preserve the broad format, not another creator's execution.
