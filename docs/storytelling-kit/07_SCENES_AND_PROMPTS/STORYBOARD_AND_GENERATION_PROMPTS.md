# Storyboard and Generation Prompts — Script 001

## Global visual style

Original polished block-style 3D game animation. Clear readable silhouettes; expressive but not overly realistic faces; cinematic lighting; controlled depth of field; coherent geography; stable character identities. 9:16 vertical composition. Keep faces and important action away from platform UI zones. Do not reproduce TeamDeam's exact designs or scenes.

## Global character continuity

**Noah:** 15-year-old block-style avatar, messy dark-brown hair, blue hoodie, stable face proportions, expressive dark eyes.  
**Lily:** 8-year-old block-style avatar, long brown hair, pink bow, pink sweater, large expressive eyes.  
**Monster:** Original shadow creature, dark blocky silhouette, subtle glowing eyes; grows incrementally as lies accumulate.

Use the supplied character reference sheets. Preserve identity in every shot.

## Scene plan

### 01 — Hook (0:00–0:08)
- Shot: tight close-up on Lily, then rack focus / reveal over Noah's shoulder.
- Action: Lily stares at the small shadow behind Noah. Noah remains unaware.
- Emotion: Lily frightened; Noah neutral/oblivious.
- Camera: slow push-in, then short reveal.
- Prompt: `Vertical 9:16 original block-style 3D animated story. Close-up of the same young girl from the reference sheet looking past the camera with frightened wide eyes. Reveal a small original shadow creature with dim eyes behind her older brother in a softly lit home hallway. The brother does not notice it. Tense cinematic lighting, readable expressions, stable character design, controlled camera push-in, no text, no logos.`

### 02 — The rule (0:08–0:22)
- Shot: quick visual demonstration with a generic adult and listener; avoid adding unnecessary named cast.
- Action: a small shadow appears only from listener's viewpoint.
- Emotion: mysterious, explanatory.
- Camera: short clean cuts.
- Prompt: `Vertical 9:16 original block-style 3D animation showing a simple visual demonstration of a lie creating a small shadow creature visible only to the listener. Keep the action clear and quick; no written text. Consistent stylized game-world design.`

### 03 — Message from Dad (0:22–0:43)
- Shot: kitchen medium shot; insert phone screen; Lily enters holding a handmade paper crown.
- Action: Noah reads the message, hides it, turns toward Lily.
- Emotion: Noah worried but masking it; Lily hopeful.
- Camera: phone insert, then two-shot.
- Prompt: `Original block-style 3D animated kitchen scene, same Noah character in blue hoodie sitting at the table looking at a phone, hiding a disappointing message. Lily enters wearing pink sweater and pink bow, holding a small handmade paper crown. Warm family lighting, subtle acting, clear hand and eye direction. Do not render legible phone text unless the provider can render exact supplied text reliably; add the message in post if needed.`

### 04 — Lily notices the creature (0:43–1:00)
- Shot: Lily's gaze shifts; over-the-shoulder reveal; Noah dismisses her.
- Action: creature grows from ankle height to knee height.
- Emotion: Lily quiet fear; Noah denial.
- Prompt: `Same kitchen/hallway continuity. Lily notices the shadow creature behind Noah and takes one small step backward. Noah dismisses her without looking at the creature. Creature grows slightly but remains visible only from Lily's viewpoint. Close-up on Lily's fearful reaction, then Noah's forced casual smile.`

### 05 — Repeated lies montage (1:00–1:27)
- Shot A: breakfast two-shot.
- Shot B: Lily shows a drawing at school or at home after school.
- Shot C: nighttime window scene.
- Action: each lie makes the same creature visibly larger.
- Emotion: hope gradually becomes unease; Noah becomes impatient.
- Prompt A: `Same two characters at breakfast, Lily asks a hopeful question, Noah gives a reassuring but slightly strained answer. Creature grows subtly in background, visible only to Lily.`
- Prompt B: `Lily proudly holds a child's drawing toward Noah. Noah gives a supportive but dishonest answer while briefly avoiding eye contact. Same outfits and face identity.`
- Prompt C: `Nighttime bedroom window, Lily asks if Dad will arrive tomorrow. Noah answers too quickly. The same shadow creature is now taller and more threatening behind him. Moonlight, quiet suspense, no jump scare.`

### 06 — Birthday and empty chair (1:27–1:49)
- Shot: wide composition of birthday table with empty chair and untouched plate; medium close-up of Lily.
- Action: Lily asks if they should wait; Noah cannot answer.
- Emotion: quiet sadness; avoid melodrama.
- Prompt: `Original block-style animated birthday room. A small cake, two children, one visibly empty chair and one untouched plate. Lily holds the cake lighter and looks between the empty chair and Noah. Same large shadow creature fills part of the hallway behind Noah. Warm birthday lights contrast with the empty chair. Slow gentle push-in.`

### 07 — Confession (1:49–2:10)
- Shot: close-up Noah; cut to Lily listening; creature cracks and dissolves.
- Action: Noah finally tells the truth.
- Emotion: guilt, vulnerability, relief beginning.
- Prompt: `Close-up of the same Noah character. His forced smile slowly disappears, his eyes lower, eyebrows draw inward, hands tremble subtly. He confesses the truth to Lily. Cut to Lily listening with sadness but no anger. The shadow creature slowly cracks and dissolves into tiny dark particles as the truth is spoken. Gentle, restrained emotional visual effects.`

### 08 — Emotional payoff (2:10–2:25)
- Shot: Lily takes Noah's hand; places paper crown on his head; final two-shot dancing beside the cake; empty chair remains visible.
- Emotion: vulnerable warmth, not exaggerated happiness.
- Prompt: `Same birthday room. Lily wipes her eyes, takes Noah's hand, and gently places her handmade paper crown on his head. They share a small sincere smile and dance beside the cake. Keep the empty chair visible in the background to preserve the emotional truth: Dad is still absent, but the siblings are together. Warm gentle lighting, slow camera drift, satisfying quiet ending.`

## Final generation notes

- Treat each prompt as a shot direction, not a substitute for the full storyboard.
- Attach the character references wherever supported.
- Reuse a location reference to preserve layout and lighting continuity.
- Avoid text in generated frames if the model renders text poorly; add precise captions using the existing workflow.
- Do not generate all scenes from one vague prompt.
- Check every output against the narration before assembly.
