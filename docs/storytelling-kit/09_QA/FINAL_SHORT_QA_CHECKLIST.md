# Final YouTube Shorts QA Checklist

- [ ] Video is vertical 9:16.
- [ ] Final file is playable and not truncated.
- [ ] Duration matches story requirements and is within the platform's current Shorts limit.
- [ ] First frame gives a clear reason to stop scrolling.
- [ ] No long logo/title intro.
- [ ] Captions are accurate, readable and inside safe margins (if captions are used).
- [ ] Voice is intelligible and consistent.
- [ ] Audio does not clip.
- [ ] Music/SFX are properly licensed for the intended use.
- [ ] No watermarks or unwanted model artifacts.
- [ ] Story is original and not a lightly altered copy.
- [ ] Video and audio commercial-use permissions have been checked.
- [ ] Owner has approved the final video in Telegram.
- [ ] Only approved videos are sent to YouTube scheduling.
