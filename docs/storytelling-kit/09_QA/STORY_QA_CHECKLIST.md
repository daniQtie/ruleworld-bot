# Story QA Checklist

Score each item Pass / Needs revision / Fail.

- [ ] The premise is understandable within the first three seconds.
- [ ] The opening creates a specific unanswered question.
- [ ] The world rule is simple enough to remember.
- [ ] The protagonist has a clear immediate goal.
- [ ] The protagonist has something personal to lose.
- [ ] The main relationship matters to the outcome.
- [ ] The conflict is caused by the rule, not coincidence.
- [ ] Each scene adds new information, tension or a decision.
- [ ] Dialogue sounds natural and distinct for each character.
- [ ] No generic filler scene or repeated exposition.
- [ ] The twist is foreshadowed and earned.
- [ ] The ending pays off the hook.
- [ ] The story is original and distinct from recent uploads.
- [ ] The final duration is appropriate to the story, not padded.

**Blocking rule:** If hook clarity, protagonist goal, causal conflict, originality or payoff fails, rewrite before video generation.
