# Character Consistency Prompt

Use the supplied character reference image as the identity anchor. Reproduce the same original block-style avatar; do not redesign the face.

Preserve exact:
- Head outline and dimensions.
- Eye shape, spacing, pupil size and placement.
- Eyebrow thickness and shape language.
- Nose and mouth proportions.
- Hairline, hairstyle silhouette and hair color.
- Skin tone.
- Outfit, clothing colors and accessories.
- Age impression and body proportions.

Only change the expression, gaze direction, head angle, body pose, lighting and camera framing as required by the shot.

Do not add extra accessories, change clothes, alter hairstyle, change the eye design, age the character, or introduce new facial features unless explicitly requested in the character bible.
