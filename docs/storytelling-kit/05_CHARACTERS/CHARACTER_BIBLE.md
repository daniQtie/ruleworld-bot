# Character Bible — Sample Story 001

These are original characters created for the sample story. The look is a general block-style game-avatar aesthetic, not a copy of a specific creator's avatar or branded character.

## Noah

- Role: Older brother and protagonist.
- Age: 15.
- Story function: Tells protective lies because he fears his sister's disappointment.
- Personality: Protective, responsible beyond his age, conflict-avoidant, quietly guilty.
- Appearance reference: `assets/character-sheets/noah_expression_reference.png`.
- Editable reference: `assets/character-sheets/noah_expression_reference.svg`.
- Hair: Messy dark brown.
- Clothing: Blue hoodie.
- Palette: Blue, warm brown, neutral denim/black accents.
- Default posture: Slightly tense shoulders; often looks away when uncomfortable.
- Speech: Natural, short sentences. He tries to sound reassuring at first, then becomes hesitant and honest.
- Arc: False reassurance → denial → guilt → truth → emotional connection.

## Lily

- Role: Younger sister and emotional point-of-view character.
- Age: 8.
- Story function: Notices the monster and understands that the lies are hurting her.
- Personality: Hopeful, imaginative, observant, emotionally honest.
- Appearance reference: `assets/character-sheets/lily_expression_reference.png`.
- Editable reference: `assets/character-sheets/lily_expression_reference.svg`.
- Hair: Long brown hair with a pink bow.
- Clothing: Pink sweater.
- Palette: Pink, warm brown and soft cream.
- Default posture: Open posture; expressive head tilts and eye focus.
- Speech: Simple, natural phrases; direct questions; no adult-sounding exposition.
- Arc: Hope → uncertainty → fear → sadness → honest connection.

## The Lie-Monster

- Role: Visual manifestation of the story's world rule.
- Design: Original shadow creature with a compact blocky silhouette, elongated but readable limbs, dim eyes that brighten as it grows, no copied game/film creature features.
- Scale progression: shoe-height → knee-height → waist-height → taller than Noah → dissolves after confession.
- Movement: Slow, subtle and unnerving. Avoid constant lunging or noisy jump scares.
- Story logic: Only Lily can see it because she is the person being lied to. Noah cannot react to something he cannot see.
- Sound: Low room tone and subtle texture; never drown out dialogue.

## Character continuity locks

Across every scene, preserve:
- Head shape and face proportions.
- Eye size, spacing and pupil style.
- Hair silhouette and color.
- Clothing, accessories and palette.
- Body scale relative to other characters.
- Voice identity if character-specific TTS is used.

If the generator supports reference images, attach the relevant character sheet or approved reference image to every scene. If it does not, repeat a concise character description in each scene prompt and inspect outputs for drift.

Do not use one character sheet for a new unrelated story. Create a new original character bible for each recurring cast.
