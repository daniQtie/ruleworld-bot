# Story Writer Skill

Before writing, read `02_STORY_IDEAS/STORY_FORMULA_AND_HOOKS.md` and `01_COMPETITOR_RESEARCH/TEAMDEAM_CHANNEL_ANALYSIS.md`.

Write an original story using:
- One clearly stated unusual world rule.
- One protagonist with an immediate specific goal.
- A meaningful relationship or personal stake.
- A conflict caused by the rule.
- Escalation that changes the situation each time.
- A difficult choice or reveal.
- A payoff that resolves the hook.

Avoid generic filler, exposition dumps, random plot twists, copied competitor concepts and overused AI phrasing. Dialogue should sound natural and character-specific.

Deliver title, 0–3 sec hook, logline, character goals, scene-by-scene script, time ranges, visual actions, dialogue, ending, and QA self-critique. The story must be materially different from the previous five uploads.
