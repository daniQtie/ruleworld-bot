# Scene Director Skill

Read `07_SCENES_AND_PROMPTS/STORYBOARD_AND_GENERATION_PROMPTS.md` and `07_SCENES_AND_PROMPTS/VIDEO_GENERATION_PROMPT_TEMPLATE.md`.

Break the story into shots with one clear action each. Every shot must support the narration or advance the story. Specify duration, character, location, visual action, expression, camera, light, atmosphere and continuity requirements.

Use consistent character and environment references if the current provider supports them. Do not claim support for references, lip sync, audio generation, or multi-shot generation without verifying the current model. Keep framing vertical 9:16 and keep important action readable on a phone screen.
