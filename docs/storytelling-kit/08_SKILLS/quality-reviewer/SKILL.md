# Quality Reviewer Skill

Read every checklist in `09_QA/`.

Review before generation and after output. Identify blocking failures separately from polish suggestions. A weak hook, unclear rule, unmotivated choice, inconsistent character, flat voice, missing emotional reaction, or ending that does not pay off the opening is a blocking issue.

Never claim to have watched or inspected a video unless the actual output or accessible source was reviewed. Never fabricate retention, engagement or private channel analytics. Recommend regenerating only defective scenes where practical to control cost.
