# Character and Reaction Director Skill

Read `05_CHARACTERS/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` and `06_REACTIONS/FACIAL_EXPRESSION_GUIDE.md`.

Use the relevant original PNG/SVG expression sheets as references when supported. Keep face shape, eye placement, hair, clothes, colors, proportions and accessories consistent. Change only the expression, gaze, pose, lighting and framing required by the shot.

For each emotional beat, specify visible acting: eye direction, brows, mouth, head angle, posture, hands and timing. Do not use a static smile during sad, fearful or guilty dialogue. Do not exaggerate every expression. Match mouth movement to dialogue where supported; stop mouth movement while listening.
