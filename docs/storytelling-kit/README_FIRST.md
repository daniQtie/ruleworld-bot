# Roblox Storytelling Claude Kit

**Purpose:** A ready-to-scan creative and quality package for producing original Roblox-style animated YouTube Shorts inspired by the *storytelling format* of TeamDeam, without copying its scripts, characters, scenes, assets, dialogue, or branding.

## Start here

1. Open `CLAUDE_SCAN_THIS_FIRST.md` and give it to Claude Code.
2. Give Claude access to the whole extracted folder.
3. Ask Claude to scan all Markdown, JSON, CSV, SVG, and PNG assets before editing your current automation.
4. Have Claude produce an audit and integration plan first. Do not let it spend generation credits or change production behavior until you approve.

## Important notes

- The included Noah/Lily story is an original draft created for this kit, not a TeamDeam script.
- The competitor metrics are dated research snapshots from November 2026 and are not live analytics or guarantees.
- The face sheets are original visual guides generated for this kit. Treat them as design references, not finished Roblox assets.
- Use the current video-generation and voice systems already configured in the owner's project. If a Three.js-based GitHub renderer is already working, audit and reuse it. This kit is provider-agnostic and does not require a hosted video-generation service.
- Do not randomly replace your existing voice ID or model. Fill in the real settings in `04_VOICE/voice_profile.json` after auditing your current setup.
- Do not delete working Telegram or YouTube integrations.
- Do not make Claude buy credits, start subscriptions, or spend generation credits without explicit approval.
- For monetization, use original stories and assets, and verify the commercial-use terms of each generation service and audio asset.
