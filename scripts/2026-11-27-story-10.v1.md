# Script v1: "In This World, Your Number Shows How Important You Will Become"

1. In this world, your number shows how important you will become.
2. Every child is born with one, floating right above their head.
3. Some kids get twelve. Some get forty-five. A few even get nine hundred.
4. My best friend Mason was born with twelve hundred and four.
5. Everyone knew he would grow up to be someone big.
6. But when I was born, the doctor looked at my number and went completely silent.
7. It was zero.
8. Growing up, everyone treated me like I would never matter.
9. At school, the kids with big numbers were treated like celebrities.
10. I tried everything to raise mine. I studied harder. I helped people. I even won competitions.
11. But my number never moved.
12. Then one day, a fire broke out at school.
13. Everyone panicked. Students were trapped inside.
14. I ran inside to help. One by one, I pulled them out.
15. The very last one was Mason.
16. When he was finally safe, I looked up and saw that my number had changed.
17. It was not zero anymore. It was infinity.
