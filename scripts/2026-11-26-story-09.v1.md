# Script v1 (Option A): "In This World, You Can See How Much Luck You Have Left"

15 lines, 162 words, about 46 s. Music: tension (original).

1. In this world, you can see how much luck you have left.
2. Everyone has a glowing number on their chest. Green means good luck. Red means bad luck.
3. One morning, a boy named Noel woke up to a blinding green one thousand.
4. He ran outside and had the best day of his life.
5. He found one hundred dollars on the sidewalk.
6. Strangers smiled and gave him compliments.
7. Then someone handed him the keys to a brand new car, for free.
8. Every time something good happened, a number somewhere else turned red. Noel never looked.
9. Late that night, he finally went home.
10. The moment he stepped inside, his chest flashed red. Minus one million.
11. His heart pounded. His little sister was crying on the kitchen floor.
12. And his mother lay beside her, clutching her chest.
13. That is when he understood. The system never gives free luck.
14. It only moves it from someone else.
15. And Noel had taken every drop from his own mother.
