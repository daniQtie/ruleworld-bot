# Our Teacher Banned Talking For A Whole Week
Hook text: NOBODY CAN ANSWER HIM | music: bounce | 90 words, about 26 s

1. Our teacher banned talking for a whole week.
2. Say one word, he warned, and you get detention.
3. So the whole class went completely silent.
4. Then he started the lesson like nothing had happened.
5. Okay, who knows the answer to this one?
6. Nobody moved. Thirty kids, just staring at him.
7. Come on! Somebody answer me!
8. Then Leo slowly raised his hand, and pointed at the board.
9. No talking. The teacher had asked a question nobody was allowed to answer.
10. The next week, he banned homework instead. And nobody said a word.
