# The Team With Nobody On It (v2)

1. Every day at recess, Leo picks the same team. The one with nobody on it.
2. He plays soccer alone against the whole class. And he loses. Every single day.
3. When Rex asked why, Leo pointed at the empty goal. That spot is taken.
4. Kids laughed. They called him Leo the Loser.
5. For a whole year, nobody stood in that goal. Leo never let anyone take it.
6. What nobody knew was why.
7. Last year, Leo's best friend Ollie was the best goalie in school.
8. Then Ollie was hurt in a car accident. The doctors said he might never come back to school.
9. Before Ollie moved away, Leo made him a promise. Your spot is waiting. I'll keep it until you come back.
10. So every day, Leo played alone. Two hundred games. Two hundred losses.
11. Then one Monday, a boy in a wheelchair rolled onto the field.
12. Leo dropped the ball and ran. Ollie! You're back!
13. Ollie looked at the empty goal. You really kept it for me?
14. Leo smiled. Every single day.
15. Rex looked at Ollie. Then at the scoreboard. Two hundred losses, all for one promise.
16. Rex walked over and stood on Leo's side. So did everyone else.
17. Ollie rolled into the goal. That day, Leo's team finally won. Ollie didn't let in a single goal.
18. Now nobody calls him Leo the Loser. They call him the kid who kept his promise.
