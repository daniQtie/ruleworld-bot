# The Town Where Broken Promises Crack The Street
Hook text: BEFORE YOU PROMISE... | hook #7 The "Before You..." Hook | format #3 The Town Where... | music: tender | 132 words

1. Before you make a promise in this town, look at the ground.
2. This is the town where every broken promise cracks the street.
3. Rex breaks promises all day. His whole street looks like a puzzle.
4. Eli has never broken one. Not a single crack.
5. Then he promised his little sister Mia he would see her sing at six.
6. At five, Rex begged him to stay for one more game.
7. Eli stayed. At six, the street split open between him and the school. Too wide to jump.
8. So he ran the long way, through the forest.
9. He burst in on the very last note. Mia saw him and smiled.
10. Back in town, the crack closed. A late promise still counts.
11. So before you make a promise in this town, keep it. Even late.
