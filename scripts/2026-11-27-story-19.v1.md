# Everyone Got A Future Card, Except Me
Hook text: MY CARD WAS BLANK | hook #8 The Story / Personal Hook | format #4 Everyone Has... Except Me... | music: tender | 121 words

1. I was the only kid in my school whose future card came out blank.
2. Everyone gets one on their first day. It tells you what you will be.
3. Zoe's card said doctor. Marcus got astronaut.
4. Mine was empty. So they called me Nobody.
5. I tried everything. Painting, cooking, soccer. I was bad at all of it.
6. Then a new kid, Ollie, opened his card. It was blank too. He started crying.
7. I sat next to him and said, blank just means nobody decided for you.
8. That night, my card finally printed one word. Teacher.
9. Blank cards wait until you do the thing you were born for.
10. Now I help the new kids open their cards. And this morning, one came out blank.
