# A Giant Tsunami Is Coming. Where Would You Hide?
Hook text: WHERE WOULD YOU HIDE? | music: bounce | 107 words, about 31 s

1. A giant tsunami is coming. Where would you hide?
2. You only have seconds to choose.
3. Option one: a big beach house with food, power and soft beds.
4. But the wave will smash the windows and flood every room.
5. Option two: a tall apartment building a few blocks back. You are high above the street.
6. But if the water reaches it, there is nowhere left to run.
7. Option three: a secret bunker deep inside a mountain, with endless food, a gaming room and a private pool.
8. The wave can never reach you there.
9. But to pick the bunker, you have to subscribe and comment which one you chose.
