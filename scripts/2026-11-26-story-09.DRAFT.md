# DRAFT: "In This World, You Can See Your Luck"

Based on the beat plan of @Gneef's Short "In This World, You Can See Your Luck" (https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ZUREX5u_c18, 41 s, 103K likes, about 105 words). Names, wording and details are new. Not approved, not rendered.

## Their beats (paraphrased, in order)
1. Rule: everyone is born with a glowing number on the chest that shows how much luck they have left today. Green is good luck, red is bad.
2. A boy wakes up to a blinding green 1,000.
3. He goes out and has the best day of his life: he finds a hundred-dollar bill, gets compliments and wins a new car.
4. He comes home. At the door his number flashes red and drops to minus one million.
5. His little sister is crying on the kitchen floor, and their mother has a heart attack.
6. The reveal: the system never gave free luck. It only moves luck from someone else. He took his own mother's luck.

## Script v1 (Option A: their dark ending, plus our additions)
1. In this world, everyone has a glowing number on their chest that shows how much luck they have left today.
2. Green numbers mean good luck. Red numbers mean bad luck.
3. One morning, a boy named Noel woke up to a blinding green one thousand.
4. He ran outside and had the best day of his life.
5. He found a hundred-dollar bill on the sidewalk.
6. Strangers smiled and gave him compliments.
7. Then a stranger handed him the keys to a brand new car, for free.
8. Every time something good happened, a number somewhere else went down. Noel never looked.
9. Late that night, he finally went home.
10. The moment he stepped inside, his chest flashed red. Minus one million.
11. His heart pounded. His little sister was crying on the kitchen floor.
12. And his mother lay next to her, clutching her chest.
13. That is when he understood. The system never gives free luck.
14. It only moves it from someone else.
15. And Noel had taken every drop from his own mother.

## Our additions (to make it stickier)
- **Clue shown early, never spoken:** in the morning shot, his mother's number is already dropping (+300, +120, +40) while his goes up.
- **Line 8:** every lucky event shows another person's number going red for a second, so the viewer guesses before he does.
- **Line 10 and the ticking sound:** a heartbeat-like tick that speeds up as his number falls, then silence on "Minus one million."

## Option B: a redemptive ending (replaces lines 13-15)
13. Noel dropped to his knees. Take it back, he begged. Take it all back.
14. His chest turned bright green again. Hers turned red, then slowly green.
15. Luck is never free. But it can always be given back.

## How the original is made (what I could check)
- **Look:** a Roblox-style 3D scene with a close, slightly low camera on the hero, plain bedroom, then street, then a kitchen. A big floating number on the hero's chest is the main graphic, and the words flash in big colored text (green, red) as they are spoken.
- **Camera:** mostly close mediums that cut every 2–3 s; a quick push-in on the number when it changes; a wide for the street.
- **Captions:** big words, one or two at a time, with the key word in color ("GREEN", "BEST DAY").
