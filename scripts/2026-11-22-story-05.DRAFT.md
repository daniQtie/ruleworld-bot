# DRAFT v2 (not approved, not QA'd): "The Smallest Kid In School Just Jumped Over The Gym"

Same beat plan as TeamDeam's #2 Short (`research/teamdeam-top2-short-analysis.md`), but with a new ability (super jump), a new event (school jump contest), new names and looks, new lines, and extra retention hooks.
Length: about 150 words, about 55 s (Rule E exception, as for episodes 4). Music: the original music box (Rule G2). Title first (Rule R). Voice: Adam, Rule Q.

## Cast (new, original)
- **Nico** (hero, the smallest kid): cyan T-shirt with a yellow lightning bolt, spiky blond hair, big red sneakers, scale 0.78.
- **Chase** (star jumper): navy-and-gold varsity jacket, slicked black hair, gold headband, scale 1.25.
- **Coach Reyes**: red tracksuit, ponytail, whistle, tape measure.
- **Nico's mum**: orange coat, long black hair (in the stands, last beat).
- A crowd of 6 distinct avatars.

## Retention hooks added (and where)
1. **Title = a promise with a "how?"**: "jumped over the gym" (line 1).
2. **A secret promise to his mum** (line 3) is an open loop. It pays off in the last line (she smiles).
3. **One jump each** (line 7) raises the stakes.
4. **Raise the rival first** (line 8): a school record that looks unbeatable, so the twist hits harder.
5. **Foreshadowing + a beat of silence** (line 10): the floor cracks under Nico's feet, then a short pause before the twist.
6. **Numbers on screen** (bar heights, the tape measure): a counter, 2.0 m record then 20.4 m.
7. **Micro cliffhangers** at about 12 s ("Nico had a secret.") and about 30 s ("Nico bent his knees.").
8. **A camera change every 2 s**, and no scene lasts more than 3 s.
9. **A loop ending**: the closing shot echoes the title, and the description asks a comment question.

## Script and shots
| # | Beat (competitor's plan + our hook) | Voice (Adam) and tone | Screen and camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | title hook (promise) | "The smallest kid in school just jumped over the gym." (confident, +2 dB) | Extreme close-up of Nico's smirking face; behind him a tiny school; a flash cut to the gym roof. |
| 2 | ability from childhood | "Ever since Nico was five, he could jump higher than any human alive." (matter-of-fact) | Medium front shot: little Nico jumps onto a kitchen table, then the roof of a shed (a warm flashback tint). |
| 3 | secret (open loop) | "But he made one promise to his mum: never let anyone see." (soft, -2 dB) | Close-up: his mum, serious, holds his face; he nods. |
| 4 | at first cool, but different | "At school, he was just the smallest kid in class, and everyone laughed at him." (sad, -3 dB) | Nico alone, shrinking in a hallway; kids point and laugh. |
| 5 | humiliation setup | "At the jump contest, everyone laughed when Nico stepped up to the mat." (mocking, amused) | Gym wide: bleachers, crowd laughing; Nico looks tiny next to a tall bar. |
| 6 | rival expected to win | "Chase, the star jumper, had won every year, and everyone knew he would win again." (a little tense) | Chase flexing in a low-angle close-up; the crowd chants his name. |
| 7 | rule: stakes | "Everyone got one jump. Only one." (tense, whisper) | Coach Reyes holds up a single finger; a sign: ONE JUMP. |
| 8 | false defeat | "Chase flew over the bar. A new school record. The crowd went wild." (rising, +2 dB) | Chase soars past 2.0 m on screen; the crowd erupts. |
| 9 | cliffhanger | "Nico barely bent his knees." (very soft, -3 dB) | Extreme close-up of Nico's sneakers on the mat; the crowd chanting fades. |
| 10 | foreshadow | "Then the floor cracked under his feet." (hushed) | Low-angle: a crack spreads under Nico's shoes; a beat of silence (0.8 s). |
| 11 | twist | "A second later, he was standing on the gym roof." (shock, +4 dB) | The camera tilts up fast to the sky; Nico lands on the roof, small against the sun. |
| 12 | silence | "The whole school went silent." (hushed, -3 dB) | Wide: still crowd, mouths open; Chase's gold headband slips off. |
| 13 | proof | "Coach Reyes measured it three times, sure her tape was broken." (disbelief, +2 dB) | Close-up of the tape measure and her face; the number blinks. |
| 14 | proof | "But it wasn't." (soft, steady) | The tape reads 20.4 m; she lowers it slowly. |
| 15 | escalation | "Nico had just jumped ten times the school record." (awe) | Scoreboard: SCHOOL RECORD 2.0 m, NEW 20.4 m. |
| 16 | payoff setup | "And from that day on, everyone knew one thing." (warm, slow) | Hallway: kids gather around Nico, smiling. |
| 17 | payoff (callback) | "Nico wasn't just the best jumper in school. He was the highest human alive." (proud, +1 dB) | Hero low-angle shot: Nico against the bright sky. |
| 18 | emotional close (loop) | "And his promise to his mum? She was watching from the stands. She just smiled." (tender, -2 dB) | Close-up of his mum, wiping a tear, smiling; the crowd cheers around her. |

## Production needs
- A gym set (wood floor, bleachers, a banner, a high-jump bar and a mat, a flat roof), a hallway, a kitchen for the flashback.
- A jump (big-arc) action with a camera tilt, a floor-crack decal, a tape measure and score display (reusing the chest-counter engine), and new cast.
