# Which Room Would You Pick To Save Your Mom?
Hook text: ONE YEAR TO SAVE MOM | music: tender | 105 words, about 31 s

1. Which room would you pick to save your mom?
2. You have to survive one full year inside it.
3. Room one is freezing. No bed, no food, and every hour it gets colder.
4. And not one person will ever come to see you.
5. Room two is pitch black. You can hear your mom calling your name.
6. But no matter where you walk, you can never reach her.
7. Room three is perfect. Your dream bedroom, endless snacks and the biggest gaming setup ever.
8. And your mom is right there with you, smiling.
9. But to pick room three, you have to subscribe and comment which room you chose.
