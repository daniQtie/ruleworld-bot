# She Whispers I Love You Every Morning, Thinking I Can't Hear
Hook text: SHE THINKS I CAN'T HEAR | music: tender | 294 words

1. Every morning on the walk to school, the prettiest girl I know whispers that she loves me.
2. She thinks I can't hear her, because of my big headphones.
3. They broke two years ago. I only wear them so nobody talks to me.
4. Her name is Hana. The shyest girl in our whole school.
5. [MONDAY] On Monday, she looked straight ahead and whispered, I love you.
6. I kept my face perfectly still. Then I walked right into a pole.
7. If she ever finds out I heard, she'll stop. So I can never react.
8. [TUESDAY] On Tuesday, she tapped my headphones and asked what I was listening to. I panicked. Rain sounds.
9. [WEDNESDAY] On Wednesday, she knew thirty facts about rain. She stayed up all night, just to have something to tell me.
10. During the fire drill, under the loud alarm, she held my hand and said I have nice eyelashes. I just nodded along to the rain.
11. [THURSDAY] On Thursday, she fell asleep on my shoulder on the bus and mumbled, one day you'll really hear me.
12. [FRIDAY] On Friday, she whispered it again. So I took my headphones off and said, I love you too.
13. She froze. You heard everything? Even the eyelashes? She turned so red she hid her face in her sleeves.
14. Then she laughed and ran across the road, shouting it so the whole street could hear.
15. For the first time ever, she wasn't shy at all.
16. She never saw the truck.
17. Her stone has a little raindrop carved on it. That was my idea.
18. I still walk to school every morning in my broken headphones.
19. And every morning at the corner, I hear her. I love you.
20. I keep my face perfectly still. If she ever finds out I can hear her, she might stop.
