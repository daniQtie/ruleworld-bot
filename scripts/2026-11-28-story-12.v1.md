# Which Pill Would You Take For A Million Dollars?
Hook text: PICK ONE PILL | music: bounce | 102 words, about 30 s

1. Which pill would you take for a million dollars?
2. A stranger hands you three pills. You must swallow one to get the money.
3. The blue pill gives you super strength.
4. But you can only use it one day a year, and after that, you are weak for a whole month.
5. The red pill lets you read every mind around you.
6. But you can never turn it off. Every thought, all day, forever.
7. The green pill gives you speed, strength and flight, with no downside at all.
8. But to take the green pill, you have to subscribe and comment which pill you chose.
