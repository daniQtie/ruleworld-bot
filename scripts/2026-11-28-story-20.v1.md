# The Secret Behind Our School's Weirdest Rule
Hook text: NEVER SAY THIS | hook #9 The "Secret / Nobody Tells You" Hook | format #5 Strange Rules and Consequences | music: bounce | 111 words

1. Nobody tells new kids the secret behind our school's weirdest rule.
2. There's a sign in every classroom. Never say, it's quiet today.
3. On Leo's first day, the class was silent. So he smiled and said it.
4. Everyone gasped. Then the recess bell rang, two hours early.
5. The board started flashing one word. Recess.
6. Then the lights started flickering.
7. The teacher spun around. Who said it?
8. Leo turned and saw the quiet girl in the back holding a tiny remote.
9. Then Pia winked at him. She built it herself, for extra recess.
10. Leo never told anyone. Now they're best friends.
11. That's the secret nobody tells new kids. And tomorrow, there's a new kid.
