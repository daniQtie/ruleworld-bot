# Grandma Pays Me To Eat Vegetables. Grandpa Pays Me Not To. (v2)

1. Grandma pays me a dollar for every vegetable I eat. Grandpa pays me two dollars not to.
2. So every dinner at their house is a war.
3. Grandma hides broccoli in my pasta. Grandpa slips me cash under the table.
4. Grandma says, eat your carrots, sweetie. Grandpa whispers, don't you dare.
5. By Friday, I had forty dollars and a very confused stomach.
6. Then, looking for cookies, I found their secret notebook under the cookie jar.
7. It had two columns. One said Grandma. One said Grandpa.
8. Under each, the same words again and again. He came. He came. He came.
9. Every single night had two check marks.
10. That's when I remembered. Before the vegetable war, I only visited them on holidays.
11. Grandpa had stopped working. Grandma's friends had moved away. Their house was quiet every night.
12. So they made up a game. A war I could only win by coming back every night.
13. It was never about the vegetables. It was about me.
14. That night, I ate all my broccoli. Then I gave Grandpa his forty dollars back.
15. I told them, you don't have to pay me. I'll come anyway.
16. Grandma started crying. Grandpa pretended he wasn't.
17. Then he leaned in and whispered, Same time tomorrow? Three dollars.
