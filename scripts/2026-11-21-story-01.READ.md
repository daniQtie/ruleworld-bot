## Script (Adam)
1. In this town, being kind is the worst thing you can do. 🎬
2. Every time you helped someone, one thing you wore jumped onto them.
3. Carry a bag, and your hat flew straight onto their head. 🎬
4. Share your lunch, and your glasses landed right on their face. 🎬
5. Hold a door, and your scarf wrapped itself around a stranger. 🎬
6. Because of this, nobody had helped anybody in years.
7. But lately, every morning, someone woke up wearing something they had never seen.
8. A pink ribbon. A yellow hair clip. Nobody knew who gave them.
9. Then one day, a boy named Milo walked through the town gate.
10. He wore five hats, three scarves, two pairs of glasses and a backpack.
11. The whole town stared. And Milo had a secret. He was looking for someone.
12. Suddenly, a small kid named Pip tripped and fell in the dirt.
13. Milo helped him up, and pop! His red cap jumped onto Pip's head.
14. Pip touched it slowly. It was the first hat he had ever owned.
15. But something was strange. Milo did not look sad at all.
16. Then Mayor Tilda, the oldest person in town, walked over with her cane.
17. She held up her bare hands. She did not own a single thing.
18. Long ago, she had helped people too, and the rule took everything.
19. She told Milo to stop, or he would end up like her. He refused.
20. The next morning, the grumpy baker dropped a huge tray of bread.
21. Milo picked up every loaf, and his green scarf flew onto the baker.
22. But the baker did not smile. He yelled at Milo to stay away.
23. Then Milo saw it. The baker already wore the red scarf Milo knitted.
24. Meanwhile, the town grew scared. Neighbours slammed their doors as Milo walked by.
25. By sunset, Milo had given away everything but his blue shirt.
26. Then he saw tiny footprints leading from every door up to the lighthouse.
27. Under the lighthouse, he found a small, dark cellar with one lantern.
28. And in the corner sat a little girl in plain grey clothes.
29. Milo froze. It was Lena, his little sister. She ran away months ago. 🎬
30. Every night, she had sneaked out to help people while they slept.
31. Each time, the rule took one thing from her and left it on them.
32. The ribbon, the hair clip, even the red scarf were all hers.
33. Lena started to cry. She thought being kind only made you lose.
34. But then, Pip ran in, still wearing his red cap. He had followed Milo.
35. He looked at Milo and said two words nobody here ever said. Thank you.
36. Suddenly, Milo's hair blazed gold, and a brand new red cap appeared.
37. That was the real rule. Kindness waited for thanks, and nobody knew to thank Lena.
38. And back home, people always said thank you. That was why Milo wore so much.
39. Pip ran down the hill and told the whole town about Lena.
40. By sunrise, the whole town had climbed the hill and thanked her.
41. Hats, ribbons and scarves rained down on her, and the grey town turned bright. 🎬
42. Last of all, a new red scarf wrapped around her neck.
43. Lena hugged her brother tight. She was finally going home.
44. But then, everyone turned to Mayor Tilda, still grey and alone.
45. So the whole town said it together. Thank you, Mayor Tilda.
46. Her hair glowed gold, and a shining golden crown appeared on her head.
47. But deep in the dark cellar, one tiny chest stayed locked.
48. And a note on it said: for the one who was helped first.
