# The Walkie-Talkie From 1995 (v2)

1. My dad gave me an old walkie-talkie instead of a phone. That night, a kid answered.
2. He said his name was Noel, and that it was 1995.
3. I laughed. But he had never heard of a phone, or the internet.
4. So every night, we talked. He was terrible at math, so I helped him with his homework.
5. He told me his biggest dream. One day, he wanted a son he could talk to every night.
6. Then one night, Noel sounded scared. A big kid named Vince took his lunch every single day.
7. I told him what my dad always tells me. Stand tall. Look him in the eye. Say no.
8. The next night, he didn't answer. Or the night after that. Or ever again.
9. I ran to tell Dad the walkie-talkie was broken.
10. But Dad wasn't surprised. He was holding an old photo.
11. It was a boy with the same yellow walkie-talkie. On the back, it said: Noel, 1995.
12. Then it hit me. My dad's name is Noel. The boy on the walkie-talkie was my dad, when he was ten.
13. Dad said, The next day, I stood tall and said no. Vince never took my lunch again.
14. I never found out who that voice was. Not until tonight.
15. So the advice my dad gave me all my life, he got it from me.
16. He hugged me and said, Thanks for the math help, son. You were the best friend I ever had.
17. His dream came true. Now Dad and I talk every night. No walkie-talkie needed.
