# Script v1: "In This World, The First Word You Say Becomes Your Superpower"

41 lines, 501 words, about 143 s. Music: piano.

1. In this world, the first word you ever say becomes your superpower.
2. A girl named Jada began with up, and now she can float.
3. A boy down the street, Leo, said no, and nothing can ever touch him.
4. That's why parents spend months whispering words like strong and fly to their babies.
5. Rex's first word was fast. He was the fastest kid in school, and he never let anyone forget it.
6. At lunch, he raced the entire fifth grade and won by a mile.
7. Then there was Marcus.
8. He didn't speak until he was almost three. And when he finally did, his first word was mine.
9. Anything he pointed at and said mine to became his, and only he could give it back.
10. So everyone was scared of him, because nobody knew what he would take first.
11. They called him the greediest kid alive.
12. Kids joked, Careful, guys, Marcus is coming for your lunch.
13. But Marcus never took anything.
14. His shoes were falling apart. He was always tired. And he had a cough that never went away.
15. Rex couldn't figure it out. One day, he walked right up to him.
16. If you can take anything you want, why don't you just take some new shoes?
17. Marcus looked at him for a second. I already took the only thing I wanted.
18. Then he walked away, coughing. Rex had no idea what that meant.
19. A few days later, Rex's new running shoes went missing in gym class.
20. Everyone turned to look at Marcus. Rex was the first to say it.
21. So you finally took some new shoes. Give them back!
22. Marcus didn't even look up. The principal pulled him out of class, and the whole school talked about it for days.
23. A week later, the shoes were found in another kid's locker. Nobody said sorry to Marcus, and Marcus never brought it up.
24. But Rex couldn't stop thinking about him.
25. Marcus's cough was getting worse. Some days he could barely climb the stairs.
26. Then he started missing school. One day. Then a whole week.
27. So what was the one thing he took? And why was he getting sicker?
28. That night, Rex went to Marcus's house to say sorry.
29. Through the window, he saw a little girl laughing in bed. Marcus's sister.
30. On her wall hung an old card that said, get well soon.
31. The doctors had given her until winter. Marcus said mine the day she got sick.
32. Now he was the one who coughed.
33. Rex knocked. Marcus, pale and shaking, whispered, Go away.
34. Rex said, Give it to me.
35. Marcus whispered, You can't outrun it.
36. Rex smiled. Watch me.
37. Marcus gave it back. The cough hit Rex, and he ran.
38. Faster than he had ever run. The sickness fell behind and faded away.
39. The greediest kid alive had only taken one thing: his sister's pain.
40. And the fastest kid in school finally had something worth running for.
41. Then Marcus pointed at Rex and said, with a smile, the word everyone feared: mine.

Top-4 additions: (1) last line, Marcus says mine with a smile; (2) clue shown, never spoken: a get-well card in Marcus's bag and a child's drawing in his notebook; (3) line 6, the race; (4) line 31, the doctors' deadline, with a cough counter on screen.
