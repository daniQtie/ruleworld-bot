# In This Town, Every Birthday Gives You A New Power
Hook text: WORST POWER EVER? | music: drift | 120 words, about 35 s

1. In this town, every birthday gives you a brand new power.
2. When Eli turned ten, he got super speed.
3. Then it was Mason's turn. He blew out his candles,
4. and a tiny bit of snow fell on the cake. That was it.
5. Everyone laughed. Snow? That's the worst power ever.
6. Then summer came, and it was the hottest day the town had ever seen.
7. The power went out. People were fainting in the street.
8. Even Eli was too tired to run.
9. So Mason closed his eyes, and made it snow. Really snow.
10. For the first time ever, it snowed in July, and the whole town cheered.
11. Now every kid in town makes the same birthday wish.
