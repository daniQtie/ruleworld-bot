# In This World, Every Step You Take Is Counted

**Hook:** #6 Question Hook · **Format:** #2 "In This World..." · **Script QA:** PASS 8.0/10 (v3) · **427 words**, expected about 2:15 with Adam

**World rule:** Everyone gets 1,000 steps a day, shown as a glowing number on the chest. At zero you freeze where you stand (awake, but unable to move) until sunrise. Steps cannot be saved; at sunrise everyone resets to 1,000. Hidden part (planted early, revealed late): when people walk holding hands, only one of their numbers goes down.

**Characters:** Kai (11, yellow raincoat, first-person narrator) · Mrs. Pell (70, purple dress, green shawl, glasses) · Keeper Marlo (old lighthouse keeper, grey beard, captain cap) · Mum (memory flash only)

**Locations:** town street at dusk → harbour → lighthouse hill in thick fog at night (existing set, plus fog, ferry and lamp beam)

"Sixty-two steps left. A hill that needs one hundred. What would you do?"  
🎬 Close on Kai's chest: a glowing 62. Tilt up past his worried face to a dark lighthouse on a foggy hill.

**2. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *mystery*  
"In this world, everyone gets one thousand steps a day."  
🎬 Town street at dusk. People walk with glowing numbers on their chests.

**3. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *mystery*  
"A glowing number on your chest counts every single one."  
🎬 Close on a walker's chest number ticking down with each step: 214, 213, 212.

**4. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *suspense*  
"When it hits zero, you freeze. You stay awake, but you can't move. Until sunrise."  
🎬 A man mid-stride hits 0. He locks in place, pale blue, frost sparkles; only his eyes move.

**5. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *mystery*  
"And you can't save them. At sunrise, everyone goes back to one thousand."  
🎬 Sunrise light sweeps the street; every number flips to 1000; the frozen man walks on.

**6. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *curiosity*  
"That evening, I saw two little kids holding hands. Only one of their numbers was going down."  
🎬 PLANT: two small kids walk holding hands. One number ticks down; the other stays still. Kai glances at them.  
⏸️ silent beat: hold on the two counters

**7. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *longing*  
"I had spent all day running to the harbour, watching for my mum's boat."  
🎬 Kai at the harbour wall, looking out at the sea; his number reads 62.

**8. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *unease*  
"I forgot about the kids. Then the fog horn sounded."  
🎬 Kai turns toward the sea. Fog rolls over the water. A horn sounds (sfx).  
⏸️ silent beat: fog horn

**9. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *worry*  
"My mum was on the night ferry. And the lighthouse was dark."  
🎬 Wide: ferry lights blur in the fog at sea; the lighthouse on the hill is dark.

**10. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *longing*

🎬 Memory flash (warm tint): Mum on a ferry deck waving up at a bright lighthouse. Back to the dark tower.

**11. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *shock*  
"At the foot of the hill stood Keeper Marlo. Frozen. Zero steps."  
🎬 Old keeper frozen mid-step on the path, pale blue, a glowing 0, the big lamp key held out in his hand.

**12. KEEPER MARLO** — *urgency*  
"Kai! Take my key. Someone has to light the lamp!"  
🎬 Marlo's eyes and mouth move inside the frozen body. Kai takes the key from his hand.

**13. KAI** — *fear*  
"I only have sixty-two steps. The path is one hundred!"  
🎬 Kai looks from his 62 up the winding path; the top is lost in fog.

**14. KEEPER MARLO** — *resolve*  
"Then don't waste a single one."  
🎬 Close on frozen Marlo; a small proud smile.

**15. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *determination*  
"So I climbed. And I counted."  
🎬 Kai starts up the dirt path, key in hand.

**16. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *tension*  
"Fifty. Forty. Thirty. Twenty..."  
🎬 The chest counter drops 50, 40, 30, 20 as the path winds; fog thickens; a cut on 20 to the next bend where it reads 12.  
⏸️ silent beat: counter ticks

**17. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *mistake*

**18. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *fear*  
"Wasted. Nine left. And I was only halfway up."  
🎬 Kai at a bend; chest reads 9; the lighthouse still far above.

**19. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *observation*  
"There sat Mrs. Pell on her bench, like every night. She never walked anywhere."  
🎬 An old woman on a bench by the path, wrapped in a shawl.

**20. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *observation*  
"She still had nine hundred steps."  
🎬 Close on her chest: a bright 900.

**21. KAI** — *pleading*  
"Mrs. Pell, please! Take the key up! You have so many steps!"  
🎬 Kai holds the key out to her and points up the hill.

**22. MRS. PELL** — *fear*  
"Up there? Alone, in the fog?"  
🎬 Mrs. Pell clutches her shawl and stares up into the fog.

**23. MRS. PELL** — *old fear*  
"I froze on this hill once, when I was a girl. All night. Nobody came."  
🎬 Memory flash (cold blue tint): a small girl frozen on the dark hill, a 0 on her chest, eyes moving, nobody around.

**24. MRS. PELL** — *refusal*  
"No. I'm not going up there."  
🎬 She looks away from him.  
⏸️ silent beat: Kai turns and keeps climbing

**25. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *despair*  
"So I went on alone. Eight. Six. Four."  
🎬 Kai climbs alone; the counter drops 8, 6, 4.

**26. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *despair*  
"I stopped. Forty-five steps to the top. Four steps on my chest."  
🎬 Kai stands still; 4 on his chest; a small sign by the path reads 45 TO THE TOP.

**27. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *dread*  
"And out in the fog, the horn sounded again. Closer to the rocks."  
🎬 Ferry lights drift toward dark rocks below the hill. Horn (sfx).  
⏸️ silent beat: horn

**28. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *choice*  
"I could go back down. Or freeze up here, in the dark."  
🎬 Kai looks down the path, then up into the fog, then down at Mrs. Pell.

**29. KAI** — *plea*  
"Mrs. Pell! You won't be alone this time."  
🎬 Kai cups his hands and calls down the hill to Mrs. Pell.

**30. KAI** — *sincere promise*  
"Even if I freeze, I'll stay right next to you."  
🎬 Kai holds out his free hand toward her, the 4 glowing on his chest.

**31. KAI** — *plea*  
"Please. Tonight, your steps could bring my mum home."  
🎬 Kai's voice breaks. Below, Mrs. Pell looks out at the ferry lights.  
⏸️ silent beat: Pell looks at the sea

**32. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *suspense*  
"For a long moment, she didn't move."  
🎬 Hold on Mrs. Pell on her bench, fog drifting.

**33. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *relief*  
"Then she stood up, climbed to me, and took my hand."  
🎬 Mrs. Pell climbs to Kai (900 ticks to 895) and takes his hand.

**34. MRS. PELL** — *warmth*  
"Then we go together. Hold on tight."  
🎬 Close on their joined hands; she smiles bravely. She has no idea what will happen to his number.

**35. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *wonder*

**41. NARRATOR (Kai)** — *reflection*  
"Steps you walk together can bring someone home."  
🎬 Final image: the lit lighthouse over the fog, the ferry coming into the harbour.
