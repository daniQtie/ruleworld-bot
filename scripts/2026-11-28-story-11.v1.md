# Ever Since He Was Five, Leo Could See When Someone Lied
Hook text: SHE LIED EVERY DAY. WHY? | music: tender | 137 words, about 40 s

1. Ever since he was five, Leo could see when someone lied.
2. A liar's face flashes bright red, just for a second.
3. But one girl at school, Pia, turned red almost every time she spoke.
4. I'm fine, she would say. Red. I don't need help. Red.
5. So Leo decided she was fake, and he stayed away.
6. One day, the teacher asked why her homework was missing. I forgot, she said. Red.
7. After school, Leo followed her home.
8. She cooked dinner, helped her little brother with his homework, and tucked him into bed.
9. Their mom wasn't home. She never was.
10. Are you okay? her brother asked. I'm fine, she smiled. Red.
11. That's when Leo understood. She wasn't fake. She was carrying everything alone.
12. The next morning, he sat next to her and said, you don't have to be fine.
