## 5 hook candidates
Outfit for all: original "City Benny" (hoodie, backwards cap, gold shades, $ chain) or Money Benny. No Rockstar IP.

**H1 two-cent jar (CHOSEN).** "If you saved two cents a day since 2013, you could buy GTA 6."
- Story: Benny starts a coin jar in 2013 while a friend laughs; montage of 13 years; pays at counter.
- Payoff: 4,811 days x 2c = $96 > $79.99 price; the wait cost under 2 cents a day. Psych: tiny habits beat last-minute panic.
- Facts: price, release date, 13.17 yr math.

**H2 GTA flu.** "If you call in sick for GTA 6, here is what it really costs."
- Story: Benny fakes a cough, boss calls back, he is caught in a hoodie on the couch.
- Payoff: a day off at $15/h is about 8 h x 15 = $120 lost, more than the $79.99 game (math: 8x15 = 120, assumed wage); psych: hype makes us pay triple.
- Facts: GTA flu trend (meme.com), price. Risk: wage is assumed, label as example; encourages skipping work.

**H3 hype brain.** "What if waiting for GTA 6 made you happier than playing it?"
- Story: Benny counts down on a calendar, glowing, then gets the game and feels flat.
- Payoff: anticipation vs reward psychology. Facts: NEEDS a verified study source (not found yet). Weak on facts.

**H4 launch-day money.** "What if GTA 5 made $3,858 every second on launch week?"
- Story: Benny sells lemonade next to a store line and is outsold by a giant cash register.
- Payoff: $1B/3 days = $333M/day = $3,858/s; one copy per second pays his year of rent. Facts: engadget. Risk: "$1B in 3 days" is retail estimate; fine if said so.

**H5 13-year wait cost.** "If you waited 13 years for GTA 6, you paid $6 a year."
- Story: Benny ages in a waiting room reading a calendar; cashier prices the wait.
- Payoff: $79.99/13.17 = $6.07 per year. Similar to H1 but less visual; flatter story.

## Why H1
Best Benny story (a jar, a friend who laughs, aging gag), clean verifiable math, matches money plus psychology, safe on IP and policy, and the "13 years" meme is already circulating. Avoid copying the rejected "$1 jar" test: this one has a time montage, a mocking friend, a counter scene and a twist (2 cents is enough; 1 cent is not).
