## Beat structure (paraphrased)
1. **0–9 s, rule hook with two quick examples:** the first word you ever say becomes your power. A girl said "up" and can float. A boy said "no" and nothing can touch him.
2. **9–13 s, why it matters:** parents spend months teaching babies words like "strong" and "fly".
3. **13–19 s, rival hero:** Henry's first word was "fast", so he is the fastest kid in school and he never lets anyone forget it.
4. **19–31 s, the feared kid:** Jonas said "mine" first, so anything he points at and says "mine" to becomes his, and only he can give it back. Everyone is scared because nobody knows what he will take.
5. **31–38 s, the label:** everyone calls him the greediest kid alive and jokes that he will take your lunch.
6. **38–43 s, a mismatch:** Jonas never takes anything. His shoes are falling apart, he is always tired, and he has a cough that never stops.
7. **43–53 s, the question:** Henry asks him why he does not just take new shoes. Jonas says he already took the only thing he wanted and walks away coughing.
8. **53–68 s, false accusation:** Henry's new shoes go missing. Everyone blames Jonas, and Henry demands them back. The principal takes Jonas out of class. A week later the shoes are found in another kid's locker. Nobody says sorry.
9. **68–80 s, cliffhanger:** Jonas's cough gets worse and he starts missing school. The last line asks what the one thing he took was and why he is getting sicker. The story is left open (a series).

## Why it works
- The rule is clear in 3 s and is shown with two examples, which makes the viewer ask "what would my word be?"
- The hero's power (speed) is set up for later, and the feared kid has a hidden reason, which gives it a mystery.
- A false accusation makes the viewer angry at the crowd, so they keep watching.
- The ending is an open question, so people comment and look for part 2.

## Their weak points (our chances to do better)
- It never answers the question, so people who want a complete story leave unsatisfied.
- Henry does nothing heroic. His power is not used.
- The "greedy" label is never turned upside down.
