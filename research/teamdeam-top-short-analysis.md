## Beat structure of the 28 s story (paraphrased)
1. **0–4 s, setup + hook:** the girls at school vote for the cutest boy. Everyone expects the obvious winner.
2. **4–7 s, expectation:** the football captain should win, and nobody looks at the quiet kid in the hood.
3. **7–12 s, underdog stakes:** the quiet kid sits alone at lunch because nobody wants to be his friend.
4. **12–14 s, twist 1:** results announced, the cafeteria goes silent, and he won.
5. **14–19 s, doubt/proof:** people laugh and call it a prank. The teachers check every vote and all are real.
6. **19–22 s, rival reaction:** the popular boys can't believe it. One girl stands up.
7. **22–28 s, reveal + moral:** the votes were not about being rich or popular: he is the only boy who treats every girl with kindness.

Pacing: one new beat about every 3–4 s, about 75 words, no filler, one reveal, and the title is the spoiler that creates the curiosity.

## Why it works

- A relatable underdog, and the viewer wants him to win.
- It is short and gets rewatched.
- The title promises the answer.
